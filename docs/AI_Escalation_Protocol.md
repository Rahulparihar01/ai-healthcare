# AI Escalation Protocol

## Purpose
This document outlines the standard operating procedure (SOP) for handling uncertain, low-confidence, or potentially erroneous outputs generated by the Clinical Intelligence Platform's AI Copilot.

## 1. "Needs Review" Flag Handling
When the Copilot confidence score drops below 80%, a **WARNING** banner is displayed to the clinician.
- **Action Required**: The clinician MUST independently verify the AI's claims against the raw source documents (Lab Reports, Visit Notes, Prescriptions) before making any clinical decisions.
- **Documentation**: The clinician should add a manual note to the patient's chart confirming the verified information, bypassing the AI summary.

## 2. Reporting Hallucinations or Missed Critical Alerts (False Negatives)
If the AI provides factually incorrect information or fails to flag a critical drug interaction/lab value:
- **Immediate Action**: Disregard the AI suggestion and proceed with standard medical protocol.
- **Reporting**: Click the "Flag Issue" button in the UI (to be implemented) or manually email the platform engineering team with the `audit_id` provided in the AI response.
- **Investigation**: The engineering team will query the `AIAuditLog` table using the `audit_id` to retrieve the exact input prompt, context, and reasoning, and adjust the system prompt or RAG retrieval logic to prevent recurrence.

## 3. Liability & Human-in-the-Loop
- The AI Copilot is an **assistive tool**, not a diagnostic authority.
- The ultimate responsibility for patient care and clinical decisions rests solely with the attending physician.
- All AI-generated alerts must be clinically correlated before action is taken.
