## 🧠 AI Clinical Intelligence & Capabilities

Once medical records are uploaded and processed, the AI continuously analyzes, structures, and understands patient data to provide intelligent clinical insights.

### 📄 AI Report Summarization
- Automatically summarize laboratory reports.
- Summarize radiology reports (MRI, CT, X-Ray, ECG, etc.).
- Summarize prescriptions.
- Summarize discharge summaries.
- Generate patient-friendly explanations.
- Generate doctor-focused clinical summaries.

---

### 📊 AI Report Analysis
- Analyze laboratory reports.
- Analyze radiology findings.
- Detect abnormal values.
- Highlight critical findings.
- Compare current reports with previous reports.
- Identify disease progression or improvement.
