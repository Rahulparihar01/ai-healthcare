# 🧠 AI Clinical Intelligence platform

The AI Clinical Intelligence platform transforms uploaded medical documents into structured clinical knowledge instead of simply storing PDFs. Every uploaded report enriches the patient's digital health profile, enabling intelligent search, automated insights, longitudinal analysis, and clinical decision support.

---

## 📄 AI Report Summary

Every uploaded report automatically generates a concise, easy-to-read summary for both doctors and patients.

**Examples:**
- Blood Test Summary
- MRI Summary
- CT Scan Summary
- ECG Summary
- Discharge Summary
- Prescription Summary
- Laboratory Report Summary

**Benefits:**
- Reduces doctor review time.
- Helps patients understand complex medical reports.
- Highlights abnormal findings and key recommendations.

---

## 📊 AI Report Comparison

AI compares historical reports to identify trends and changes over time.

**Examples:**
- Compare CBC reports.
- Compare cholesterol reports.
- Compare kidney function.
- Compare liver function.
- Compare MRI findings.
- Compare blood sugar trends.

The system automatically highlights improvements, deteriorations, and clinically significant changes.

## 🔍 Semantic Medical Search

Doctors can search patient records using natural language instead of manually browsing reports.

**Example Queries:**
- Show all diabetes reports.
- Show MRI reports from 2025.
- Find all blood reports with high cholesterol.
- Show all cardiac medications.
- Find abnormal ECG reports.
- Show all kidney function tests.
- List all prescriptions containing Metformin.

Semantic search combines structured database filtering with vector similarity search for highly relevant results.

## 🤖 AI Clinical Assistant (RAG)

A conversational AI assistant powered by Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) enables intelligent interaction with patient records.

### Doctor Assistant
- Summarize patient history.
- Explain laboratory reports.
- Compare reports over time.
- Highlight abnormal findings.
- Generate visit summaries.
- Generate discharge summaries.
- Identify medication conflicts.
- Show previous diagnoses.
- Explain MRI, CT, ECG, and laboratory findings.

### Patient Assistant
- Explain medical reports in simple language.
- Explain prescribed medicines.
- Show medication schedules.
- Summarize recent visits.
- Explain diagnosis and treatment plans.
- Answer health-related questions using only authorized patient records.
